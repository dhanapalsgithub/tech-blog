Google AdSense is still the fastest way to monetize a **content-first** blog. The trick is to place units in a way that respects both the reader and Google's policies.

## Rules of Thumb

- Never above the H1
- Ideally *after* the first meaningful paragraph
- One in-article block, one at the end
- Never inside sticky headers or modals

## The Reusable Component

```tsx
<AdSlot slot="1234567890" format="horizontal" />
```

Under the hood the component:

1. Only initializes when it enters the viewport
2. Falls back to a **Blueprint placeholder** in development mode
3. Accepts `format`, `slot`, `label`, and `responsive` props

## Layout Guidance

| Position | Format | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| After intro | `horizontal` | High viewability |
| Mid-article | `rectangle` | Blends with content |
| End of post | `horizontal` | Captures completed readers |
